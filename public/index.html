About me

From Mechanics to Player Experience

I am a Game Designer with a Software Engineering degree and hands-on experience across mobile casual and puzzle titles. My unique value sits at the intersection of technical prototyping and cross-functional design communication — I can prototype mechanics in Unity/C#, validate them with data and playtesting, and hand them off cleanly to engineering and art.

At Rainbow5s, I analyzed gameplay telemetry to tune difficulty curves, balanced in-game economies and booster systems, and designed levels for multiple live mobile titles. I author Game Design Documents (GDDs) to align stakeholders and iterate rapidly based on player feedback.

My design practice is grounded in Gameplay Mechanics, System Design, Level Design, and Game Balancing. Every decision is driven by player data, validated through playtesting, and refined through iteration — ensuring mechanics serve the player experience first.
